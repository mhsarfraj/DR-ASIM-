Frequently asked questions

Is my appointment confirmed immediately?

No. Online submissions are requests. Your booking is confirmed only after the clinic approves it. You can check the status using your reference ID.

What are the clinic timings?

Monday to Sunday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Any temporary closure or special hours will be shown in the clinic status on this page.

What if my preferred slot is unavailable?

Choose another slot, or call or WhatsApp the clinic and we will help you find a suitable time.

Is this website for medical emergencies?

No. In an emergency, please go to the nearest hospital immediately. This website does not replace a medical consultation.

How is my personal information handled?

Only the details needed to manage your appointment are collected and stored privately. See our privacy notice.
